# String file for the Rediscover Releases plugin

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES
	EN	Rediscover Releases

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_DESC
	EN	A Material skin home row of random releases that prefers albums with no recent plays.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_ABOUT
	EN	This plugin provides a list of random releases that follow a well-defined criteria: it prioritizes albums with no recent playbacks, supports restricting the genre list, lets you choose how often the list is regenerated, and offers deep configuration. It only supports the Material skin. The list persists for a configurable number of hours; releases on it that get played in full are removed until the next regeneration.<br><br>After adding this plugin, enable the new <b>Rediscover Releases</b> row in Material's <i>Settings &gt; Interface</i> (home screen rows) and set the desired position. You can then save that setup as the default for all Material clients and apps.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_STATUS
	EN	Current list

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_STATUS_LIST
	EN	%s releases, built %s. Next regeneration: %s.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_STATUS_POOLS
	EN	At the last build the library had %s played releases not played within the period, %s played within it and %s unplayed; the list got %s unplayed ones.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_STATUS_NONE
	EN	No list yet. It is built a minute after the server starts (or after a library scan finishes).

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_REGENERATE
	EN	Regenerate List Now

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_REGENERATED
	EN	The list has been regenerated.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_COUNT
	EN	Number of releases to include in the Rediscover list

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_COUNT_DESC
	EN	10 to 1000. The list can be shorter if the library doesn't have enough releases that match.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_MONTHS
	EN	Prioritize inclusion of releases not played for the last

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_MONTHS_DESC
	EN	1 to 99 months. A release counts as played on the date of the oldest last play among its tracks, so it is only "recently played" once all of it has been heard recently. Releases played within this period are only used when there aren't enough others.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_MONTHS_UNIT
	EN	months

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_UNPLAYED
	EN	Include unplayed releases in the list

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_UNPLAYED_DESC
	EN	Releases none of whose tracks have been played (and, depending on the setting below, partially played ones).

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_MINPLAYED
	EN	Ensure that at least this share of the list is already-played releases

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_MINPLAYED_DESC
	EN	0 to 100 %. The rest of the list is picked at random from played (not recently) and unplayed releases together.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_GENRES
	EN	Include the selected genres

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_GENRES_DESC
	EN	A release is included if any of its tracks has one of the selected genres. The number after each genre is its number of releases.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_ALL_GENRES
	EN	All genres

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_PARTIAL
	EN	Partially played releases are assumed as

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_PARTIAL_DESC
	EN	Releases with some, but not all, tracks played. As played, the date used is the oldest last play among the played tracks.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_AS_UNPLAYED
	EN	Unplayed

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_AS_PLAYED
	EN	Played

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_HOURS
	EN	Rebuild the Rediscover list every

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_HOURS_DESC
	EN	1 to 999 hours. Saving changed list settings also rebuilds it.

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_HOURS_UNIT
	EN	hours

PLUGIN_REDISCOVER_RELEASES_EMPTY
	EN	The Rediscover list is empty.
